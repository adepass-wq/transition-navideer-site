Responsible interpretation

Study the journey. Do not overclaim the person.

Transition Navideer can make exploration behavior and planning milestones visible. The evidence is most useful when its limits are explicit.

Career Decision Intelligence

A lens for observable exploration over time.

Career Decision Intelligence (CDI) is the careful interpretation of what people search, compare, save, revisit and move into action planning. A parallel Transition Development Intelligence framework is still being developed and is not represented here as validated.

Expression

Initial interests, titles, constraints and questions

Exploration

Breadth of occupations and information considered

Comparison

Repeated evaluation of options and tradeoffs

Narrowing

Fewer targets receiving deeper attention

Investigation

Preparation, education, credentials and context reviewed

Action planning

Targets and development steps moved into My Career Path

Use evidence in combination

Behavior alone is incomplete.

Responsible program evaluation triangulates descriptive usage patterns with interviews, survey responses, advising context and milestone information.

Interpret carefully

  • Report time period, participant context and data coverage.
  • Describe patterns rather than inventing motivation.
  • Separate product activity from employment outcomes.
  • Protect privacy, consent and appropriate access.
  • Do not invent significance tests or validation claims.
Current evidence base

What can be said today.

Existing Transition Navideer sources include beta feedback, stakeholder interviews, participant comments and internal product observations. They support product learning and careful description, not causal claims.

Participant feedback

Selected users described the comparison tools as understandable and valued the way the platform helped organize skills and thoughts.

Professional feedback

Counselors and workforce professionals reported that the structure could support career-guidance conversations and reveal alternative options.

Known learning need

Beta feedback showed that individuals often need plain-language explanations of knowledge, skills and abilities before using the comparison well.

Limits

The current public evidence does not establish predictive validity, employment impact, mastery, psychological traits or guaranteed fit.

A credible product explains both its promise and its boundaries.
