Where it fits

A decision-support layer before, during and between advising.

Transition Navideer can support workforce boards, community colleges, universities, veterans programs, employers, outplacement providers and reskilling initiatives.

BEFOREParticipants arrive clearer.

They bring target directions, comparison questions and a more visible picture of transferable experience.

DURINGAdvisors focus on tradeoffs.

Use the conversation for fit, readiness, local realities and the evidence a participant still needs.

BETWEENAction stays visible.

Saved sessions and My Career Path keep preparation and next steps from disappearing after the meeting.

For counselors and coaches

Let the platform handle structured comparison so human conversations can concentrate on meaning, context and judgment.

For workforce and education leaders

Connect career exploration with credentials, programs and development priorities while keeping interpretation responsible.

For employers and outplacement partners

Support mobility and transition planning without representing the product as a hiring, placement or performance-prediction system.

For military transition programs

Make service experience more legible in civilian career contexts and support coordinated planning for service members and spouses.

Responsible implementation

Program design should address consent, privacy, accessibility, data governance and interpretation boundaries. Usage patterns can help programs ask better questions; they should not be treated as psychological measures or proof of readiness.
