Make your experience legible across occupations.

The live platform begins with a role and surfaces the knowledge, skills and abilities commonly associated with that work. You can refine what should carry into the comparison.

Supplemental questions add context

Income, schedule, education willingness, location, preferred activities and urgency help frame the investigation.

Occupation reports add reality

Review tasks, tools, values, work styles, preparation, earnings, outlook, education, certifications and related occupations.

Saved sessions support iteration

Return to a transition as you gather evidence, talk with people and learn more about the work.

My Career Path keeps action visible

Organize target occupations, development activities, mentors, self-care, time management and next steps.
