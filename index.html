A consequential change deserves structure

You know you need a change. The harder question is: change to what?

Adults rarely begin with a blank slate. You begin with years of work, service, responsibilities, preferences and constraints. Transition Navideer makes that history useful.

01Your field changed around you.

You need a near-term direction that uses what already transfers and shows where focused development may help.

02Your work no longer fits.

You want to compare adjacent careers without reducing the decision to a title, salary or personality label.

03You are returning or relocating.

You need a route that respects schedule, caregiving, training time, income needs and geographic realities.

Evidence with boundaries

Observe the journey without overinterpreting the person.

Career Decision Intelligence means careful interpretation of observable exploration behavior across time - not a diagnosis, personality score or prediction of success.

  • Better questions for advising

What they do not prove
