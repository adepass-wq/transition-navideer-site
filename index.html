The transition gap

Experience is abundant.
Direction is scarce.

People rarely begin a career transition with a blank slate. They begin with years of work, service, responsibilities, preferences, and constraints—but no reliable way to turn that history into a realistic next move.

Most tools enter the journey after the hardest decision has already been made.

The action layer

Exploration matters when it leads to action.

My Career Path turns a target direction into a living plan. People can organize preparation, track next steps, and revisit decisions as their transition evolves.

Self-assessment and activity completion support reflection and follow-through; they do not guarantee mastery or employment outcomes.

Where it fits

Connected to the ecosystem. Clear about its role.

Works alongside

  • Advisors, counselors, and coaches
  • Job boards and employer career sites
  • Education and credential resources
  • Résumé and application tools
  • Workforce and transition programs

Does not claim to

  • Predict fit or employment outcomes
  • Replace professional guidance
  • Hire, place, or retain candidates
  • Generate finished résumé bullets
  • Prove mastery from self-assessment
